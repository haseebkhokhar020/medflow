# Medicine catalogue data notices

The compiled `rxterms-202609.json.gz` contains a transformed subset of the
September 2026 NLM RxTerms dataset, which is free to use and derived only
from the non-proprietary content of RxNorm. Attribution: U.S. National Library
of Medicine, Lister Hill National Center for Biomedical Communications,
RxTerms, September 2026. https://lhncbc.nlm.nih.gov/MOR/RxTerms/
Data.gov lists the RxTerms dataset under ODbL; data-reuse terms for this
database are separate from the all-rights-reserved MedFlow software licence.
https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/rxterms

The small `pakistan.json` table contains manually curated identification
facts about Panadol variants published by Haleon Pakistan and Haleon
HealthPartner Pakistan. Product imagery and page text are not republished.
https://www.haleon.pk/our-brands/panadol/

Neither dataset is a DRAP registration list, a current market availability
list, a price list, a dosing guide, or evidence of regulatory approval.
Always check the physical pack and applicable local requirements.
See ../../docs/MEDICINE_CATALOG.md for provenance and limitations.
